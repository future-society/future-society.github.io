    Method and coding

    Targets (x-axis) are domain: objective pairs, like the paper's sector: objective. There are six domains: General (all AI systems), GPAI models, Products (AI in regulated products, AI Act Annex I), Public sector (law enforcement, migration, justice, democratic processes, public benefits), Private uses (employment, education, credit, insurance, critical infrastructure) and Platforms (recommender systems, moderation, online ads). Each domain is crossed with four objectives: safety & security, fundamental rights, information integrity and innovation & uptake.

    Instruments (y-axis) are 23 types. The first 14 run from command-and-control at the top to support, soft-law and governance instruments. The 9 below them are further types from general policy-instrument typologies (market-based, procurement, competition, trade and labour instruments), added to expose blind spots; most are empty at EU level. As in the paper, every instrument can in principle address every target, so the space is symmetrical: 24 × 23 = 552 boxes. Adding the further types lowers portfolio size relative to a 14-row space; it does not change which boxes are in place.

    Intensity (2–10) stands in for the stringency score of the OECD Climate Actions and Policies Measurement Framework (CAPMF). Each provision scores the sum of three parts. The box shows the highest score among its provisions.
