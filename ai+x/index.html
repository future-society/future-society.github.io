Who controls the data AI acts on?

AI assistants and agents increasingly read and act on our personal data. Solid keeps that data in a Pod its owner controls, and lets each application, AI included, use it only with permission that can be revoked.

  • Data separate from the AI that uses itYour data lives in a Pod you choose: self-hosted, at your institution, or with a provider you trust.
  • Access granted and revokedThrough open W3C standards, one application at a time.
  • No lock-inSwitch assistant or Pod provider without losing your data.
