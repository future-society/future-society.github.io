## Draft post text

Today we had the pleasure of welcoming our colleagues from Japan to the University of St.Gallen for a full day of shared research on "Computing for Reality Mediation." 🇯🇵🇨🇭

The Internet of Realities (IoR) Project team — Takuro Yonezawa (Nagoya University), Akira Kanaoka (Toho University), Manabu Tsukada (University of Tokyo), and student researchers from Nagoya and Tokyo — joined our Interactions Research Group (Simon Mayer, Jannis Strecker-Bischoff, Danai Vachtsevanou, Aurelia Tamò-Larrieux, Jake Stein) for a day of keynotes, talks, and genuinely energizing discussion.

Yonezawa opened with a keynote on "Reality Mediation: New Computing Agenda," followed by talks on trustworthy realities, software-defined environments, and medical applications in virtual and mixed reality. In the afternoon, the conversation turned to personalized realities, AI interfaces, AI-generated imagery, and remote communication — exactly the questions Jannis has been exploring in his own work on Personalized Reality, and a preview of the themes we'll bring to the International Workshop on Reality Mediation at UbiComp/ISWC 2026 in Shanghai this October.

It's easy to research these questions in isolation. It's much better to sit in a room with people asking the same questions from a different continent, a different culture, and a different set of assumptions — and to leave with new collaborations, not just new slides.

Thank you to @Nagoya University, @Toho University, and @University of Tokyo for making the trip, and for such a warm and thoughtful exchange. We're already looking forward to the next one.

🔗 Our work on Personalized Reality: https://futuresociety.ch/#focus
🔗 Personalized Realities paper: https://doi.org/10.1145/3715336.3735709
