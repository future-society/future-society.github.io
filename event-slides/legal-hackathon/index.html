Why Are You Here Today?

A legal hackathon gives you a unique opportunity for

  • Learning by doing: law is usually taught through books and cases. Here you apply what you know to real challenges, experiment, fail, iterate and build something tangible.
  • Breaking down disciplinary silos: law students work with computer scientists, designers and entrepreneurs, learn each other's language and solve problems together.
  • Shaping the future of legal services: AI is changing the legal profession. Help shape that change, so that legal services become more accessible, efficient and human-centred.

Judging Criteria

  • Problem and motivation: What legal or law-related problem are you addressing, and why does it matter?
  • Proposed solution: What is your idea, and how does it address the identified problem?
  • Prototype or service demonstration: Show how your solution works in practice, including the user experience and key functionalities.
  • Legal and technical considerations: How does your solution integrate legal and technical aspects? What are its limitations, and how do you address relevant risks?
  • Value and outlook: What makes your solution innovative or valuable, and how could it be further developed?
