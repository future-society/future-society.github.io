You're invited to the Solid Symposium 2027‹09 / 12›

Control over the data AI acts on

AI assistants and agents increasingly read and act on our personal data. Solid keeps that data in a Pod its owner controls, and lets each application, AI included, use it only with permission that can be revoked. Come and discuss it with us at the Solid Symposium in St.Gallen.

  • Your data lives in a Pod you choose, separate from the AI that uses it: self-hosted, at your institution or with a provider you trust.
  • Each application gets access through open W3C standards, and you can revoke it at any time.
  • You can switch assistant or Pod provider without losing your data.
