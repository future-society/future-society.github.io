Technology  ×  Society

Humans should not be passive consumers of technological change, but should actively architect it from a societal perspective. This is what the Future Society Hub sets out to enable and support. We serve as a dynamic problem-solving space where researchers, organizations, and citizens shape technology around human values. A proactive stance that anticipates technology-induced social shifts and shapes technology through a societal lens.

About

Addressing the pressing issues of technology requires fusing disciplines.

FuSo is a hub and point of contact that addresses the opportunities and risks of technology from start to end, grounded on the newest scientific insights.

Based at University of St.Gallen, we link expertise from the School of Computer Science and Law School to deliver evidence-informed, full-stack solutions for academics, public administrations, NGOs, and industry.

Full-Stack: From Insight to Impact

Where other hubs stop at research, FuSo accompanies stakeholders across the entire journey: ideating, co-creating, mitigating risks, implementing, and evaluating in the real world.

Insight › Policy Analysis Risk Assessment › Co-Creation Implementation › Impact Assessment User-Centered Evaluation › Policy Response Deployment › Impact

Focus Areas

Focus Areas

01

Technology Assessment

Consulting authorities on AI governance and providing regulatory expertise on emergent technologies.

02

Systemic Risk Mitigation

Implementing techno-legal tools to identify and mitigate systemic risks of emerging technologies and online platforms at global scale.

03

Education & Digital Literacy

Forecasting critical skills, shaping educational pathways, fostering new competencies, and nurturing a safe digital environment for young people online.

04

Infrastructure for the Future

Supporting the architecting, development, and regulation of future digital infrastructure to benefit society.

05

Access to Justice

Investigating the potentials of automating legal processes for greater access to law.

06

Inclusive and Healthy Society

Promoting resilient, healthy, and inclusive society through Ubiquitous Computing at scale.
